Sponsor a marathon

Put your name on one of the marathons

I am inviting local businesses to sponsor a marathon. The length of the River Thames is over 7 marathons long, and they will become progressively harder!

What it means

Where your name goes

On the river

Your name sits beside your stretch on the tracker, before and after the run.

On the shirt

Your logo can be printed on the shirt I wear for your marathon — I have to change shirts every marathon anyway.

In the updates

You get a mention in the update from your part of the river, and in anything shared from it.

Where it goes

Every pound goes to the My Name'5 Doddie Foundation, for MND research.

The seven

Choose your stretch

The later marathons are the harder ones, through the night and into London.
